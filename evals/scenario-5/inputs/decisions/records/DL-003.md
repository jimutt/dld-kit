## Decision

All outbound calls to the payment gateway use exponential backoff with full jitter. The initial delay is 100ms, the maximum delay is 30s, and the maximum number of attempts is 5. Only transient errors (HTTP 429, 503, network timeouts) trigger retries. Terminal errors (HTTP 400, 402, 422) are returned immediately without retry.

The retry policy is encapsulated in `GatewayClient` so callers never implement their own retry loops.

## Rationale

Exponential backoff reduces thundering-herd pressure on the gateway during outages. Full jitter (random delay between 0 and the computed cap) prevents synchronized retry bursts from multiple service instances. Centralizing the policy in `GatewayClient` prevents inconsistent per-call retry implementations.

## Consequences

New outbound integrations must use `GatewayClient` or replicate this policy exactly. Error codes must be classified as transient or terminal before any new downstream call is added.
