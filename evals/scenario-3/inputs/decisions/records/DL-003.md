## Context

After launch, several endpoints were hammered by misconfigured clients, causing degraded performance for other users. DL-001 established the FastAPI layer but did not specify rate limiting.

## Decision

Amend DL-001 to include a rate limiting middleware requirement. All endpoints defined in `src/api.py` must be protected by the `SlowAPI` middleware, configured at 100 requests per minute per IP.

## Rationale

Rate limiting at the middleware level ensures consistent protection without per-endpoint boilerplate. SlowAPI integrates natively with FastAPI.

## Consequences

- The SlowAPI dependency must be added to requirements.
- All endpoints inherit the default rate limit; per-endpoint overrides are allowed via decorator.
