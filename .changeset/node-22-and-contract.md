dld-kit now needs Node.js 22 or newer; Node 20 reached end-of-life in April 2026. From 1.0.0, dld-kit supports the Node.js lines in active or maintenance LTS and may drop one in a minor release once it reaches end-of-life. The semver contract is now stated in full in `docs/releasing.md`: decision records, annotations, `dld.config.yaml`, the setup and CI commands, the skill names and the install channels. Frontmatter fields dld-kit doesn't know are kept and ignored.
